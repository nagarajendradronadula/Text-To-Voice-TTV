## Training Data Structure
- **Short phrases**: 1-5 words for natural interjections
- **Medium sentences**: 6-15 words for conversational flow  
- **Long passages**: 20+ words for sustained tone consistency
- **Varied punctuation**: Questions, exclamations, pauses
- **Natural speech patterns**: Contractions, hesitations, emphasis
